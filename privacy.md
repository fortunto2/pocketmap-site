# PocketMap Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-01. Applies to the PocketMap iOS app (co.superduperai.pocketmap)._

**App Privacy label: Data Not Collected.** PocketMap has no account, no server of its own,
no analytics and no advertising. We, the developer, receive nothing from the app.

## Location

Your location is used on the phone only, to show where you are on the map and to follow
you. It is never sent anywhere, by us or by the app. You can deny the permission and
everything else keeps working.

## Network requests the app makes

The app talks to exactly two public OpenStreetMap services, and nothing else:

- **OpenFreeMap** (`tiles.openfreemap.org`): map style and tiles, when you view the map
  online or download a region for offline use. Requests contain the tile coordinates,
  which reveal the area you are looking at, and your IP address, as any web request does.
  See [openfreemap.org](https://openfreemap.org).
- **Nominatim** (`nominatim.openstreetmap.org`): only when you submit a search. The request
  contains the text you searched for and your IP address. See the
  [OSMF privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

Your GPS position is not part of either request.

## What lives on the phone

Downloaded map regions and imported GPX tracks are stored in the app's own storage.
Deleting a region in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

## Data we collect

None. There is no crash SDK and no analytics. TestFlight crash reports and feedback, if you
choose to send them, reach us through Apple only.
